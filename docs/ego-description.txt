Smooth, cursor-anchored desktop zoom for GNOME. Hold Super + Alt and scroll.

Zoom glides continuously instead of jumping in steps. The content under the cursor stays put, and GNOME's mouse tracking keeps working, including Pushes Contents Around. Zoom out to 1.00× to turn Desktop Zoom off.

Features
- Smooth, proportional zoom for mouse wheels and touchpads
- Cursor-anchored full-screen zoom
- Equal Push margins on all four edges (0–200 px, independent of zoom level)
- Multiple monitors: zoom only the monitor under the pointer; the zoom follows the pointer to the next monitor
- Camera page for GNOME mouse, keyboard focus and text cursor tracking (Push, Proportional, Centered, None)
- Adjustable shortcut, speed, smoothing and maximum zoom

Notes
- Tracking and desktop-limit controls edit shared GNOME Accessibility settings. They persist after disabling the extension.
- Disabling the extension keeps the current zoom.
- Tested on GNOME 46 Wayland, with one monitor and with two mismatched monitors. X11 and mixed scaling are not yet tested.

Based on Deperto by Dennis Guimarães (GPL-3.0). Source, issues and changelog: https://github.com/jinkim0823/smooth-push-zoom
